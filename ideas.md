# Ra'ayin Zane: Bese26 Business Directory

## Alkiblar da aka zaba
Mai amfani ya bukaci a sauya Business Directory ya zama **premium kuma mai matukar kyau**. Alkiblar ita ce shafin gano shaguna na Bese26 mai tsafta, mai nuna amana, kuma mai armashi—ba sabon tsarin alama ba.

## Tsarin zane
- **Design movement:** Premium editorial commerce; saukin karantawa, tsari mai natsuwa, da alamomin amana bayyanannu.
- **Ka'idoji:** A fara da abin da mai amfani zai nema; a nuna sunan kasuwanci da tambarinsa; kada a cunkushe bayanai; a ba kowane kati isasshen fili; a kiyaye saukin amfani a waya da manyan allo.
- **Launuka:** A daidaita dukkan app da tsarin Bese26 na baki/charcoal, fari, da ja; a cire shuɗi, purple da cyan na ado daga manyan hero, tabs, da icons marasa ma'ana ta musamman. A bar kore don WhatsApp/ingantaccen status, zinariya don taurari da premium, launukan Google/abokan hulɗa su tsaya, sannan a yi amfani da off-white da soft red don surfaces da focus.
- **Ka'idar launin maballai:** Babban aiki da wallafawa su yi amfani da ja na Bese26; maɓallan secondary, baya, da soke aiki su kasance neutral; WhatsApp ya kasance kore; matakin da aka kammala ya nuna kore; aikin haɗari ko sharewa ya nuna ja; zaɓaɓɓun tabs/filters su yi amfani da soft red. A bar maɓallin wani kamfani kamar Google da alamar kamfaninsa, kuma a kiyaye rubutu da aikin kowane maɓalli.
- **Tsarin shimfida:** Hero mai karfi da search a wuri guda; jerin sakamako a grid mai cards biyu a manyan allo da card daya a wayoyi; card ya nuna logo, suna, handle, verification, wuri da hanyar shiga miniweb.
- **Abubuwan sa hannu:** Search field mai dadi, count na sakamakon da aka samu, logo frame square mai `contain`, badge na ID/CAC, location row, da CTA mai launin Bese26.
- **Mu'amala:** Search din yanzu ya ci gaba da aiki kamar yadda yake. Cards/CTA suna da focus bayyananne, hover mai sauki, da matsayi mai kyau ga keyboard da touchscreen.
- **Animation:** Saukin transitions na gajeren lokaci kawai; kada a sa motsi ya hana mai amfani karantawa.
- **Tsarin rubutu:** A ci gaba da amfani da font stack na app; manyan taken su kasance masu nauyi da tsabta, secondary text ya zama karami amma a iya karantawa.
- **Asalin alama da murya:** Bese26 ya kasance marketplace na gida mai saukin amfani, amana, da kwarewa. A yi amfani da tambarin da ke cikin app; ba a bukatar sabon logo ko hoton waje.
- **Sa hannun launi:** Ja na Bese26 a matsayin primary CTA da active/focus accent, kore don WhatsApp da alamar kammala aiki kawai, tare da baki/fari don secondary da neutral actions.
