# Jagorar Amfani da Business Admin na Bese26

**Sigar jagora:** 1.0  
**Harshe:** Hausa  
**Manufa:** Wannan jagora tana bayyana yadda mai amfani zai kirkiri, saita, ya sarrafa, kuma ya raba Business Storefront dinsa a Bese26.

## 1. Menene Business Admin?

**Business Admin** shi ne bangaren kula da shago na Bese26. Yana ba mai kasuwanci damar sarrafa bayanan business dinsa, listings, bayanan tuntuɓa, storefront link, verification, da wasu alkaluman aiki daga cikin account guda.

Ba a bukatar sabon login domin amfani da Business Admin. Personal account din mai amfani shi ne account din da yake mallakar business din.

> Bayan an kirkiri business profile, maballin **Upgrade to Business** yana komawa **Business Admin**. Daga nan mai business zai iya komawa dashboard din kula da shagon a duk lokacin da yake so.

## 2. Abubuwan da ake bukata kafin farawa

Kafin ka fara, ka tabbatar cewa kana da account a Bese26 kuma ka shiga daidai. Haka kuma yana da kyau ka tanadi bayanan business kamar yadda aka tsara a kasa.

| Bayani | Dalilin amfani da shi |
|---|---|
| Sunan business | Sunan da buyers za su gani |
| Business handle | Yana samar da public link kamar `www.bese26.shop/@mekano` |
| Category da business type | Yana taimaka wa buyers su fahimci irin business din |
| Short description | Bayanin abin da business din ke sayarwa ko yi |
| Phone da WhatsApp | Hanyoyin da buyer zai tuntubi business |
| State da city/LGA | Bayanin wurin business |
| Logo | Hoton da zai wakilci business |
| Pickup ko delivery | Hanyar da buyer zai karbi kaya |

## 3. Yadda ake kirkirar Business Profile

### Mataki na farko: Shiga account

1. Bude [Bese26.shop](https://www.bese26.shop/).
2. Shiga account dinka.
3. Je zuwa **Profile**.
4. Danna **Business Center** ko **Upgrade to Business**.

### Mataki na biyu: Cika Business Identity

A sashe na farko, cika wadannan bayanai:

- **Business name:** Sunan shagonka ko kamfaninka.
- **Category:** Misali `Phones & Electronics`, `Fashion`, `Agriculture`, ko `Services`.
- **Public handle:** Karamin sunan da zai kasance a link. Yi amfani da kananan haruffa, lambobi, da hyphen kawai.

Misali:

```text
Business name: Mekano Electronics
Business handle: mekano
Public link: https://www.bese26.shop/@mekano
```

Tsarin zai duba ko handle din yana samuwa. Idan aka nuna **Handle already taken**, zabi wani handle daban.

### Mataki na uku: Bayanan tuntuɓa da wurin business

A mataki na biyu, saka:

- Short description na business.
- State.
- City ko Local Government Area.
- Phone number, idan kana son a kira ka.
- WhatsApp number, idan kana son buyers su tuntuɓe ka a WhatsApp.
- Hanyar tuntuɓa da kake so a nuna.

| Zabi | Abin da buyer zai gani |
|---|---|
| Show WhatsApp and Call | WhatsApp da Call |
| WhatsApp only | WhatsApp kawai |
| Call only | Call kawai |

Ka rubuta description a takaice, cikin bayani mai amfani. Misali: **Muna sayar da wayoyi da kayan computer, kuma muna karbar pickup a Kano.**

### Mataki na hudu: Dubawa da adanawa

A mataki na karshe, duba bayanan da ka shigar. Idan komai ya yi daidai, danna **Create business profile** ko **Save changes**.

Bayan an adana bayanan, public storefront link zai kasance a tsarin:

```text
https://www.bese26.shop/@business-handle
```

## 4. Yadda ake shiga Business Admin bayan an kirkiri business

1. Shiga Bese26.
2. Bude **Profile**.
3. Danna **Business Admin**.
4. Za ka ga dashboard din business dinka.

Idan ba ka ga Business Admin ba, ka tabbatar cewa kana cikin account din da ya kirkiri business profile din. Business Admin yana nuna bayanan business din mai account ne kawai.

## 5. Bayanin manyan sassan Business Admin

### 5.1 Overview

Overview yana nuna yanayin business cikin sauri. A nan za ka iya ganin:

- Yawan views.
- Buyer inquiries.
- Sold listings.
- Active listings.
- Verification status.
- Current plan.
- Public page status.

Wadannan alkaluma suna taimaka maka ka gane ko buyers suna ganin shagonka da listings dinka.

### 5.2 Manage Listings

Danna **Manage listings** domin zuwa bangaren listings. A nan za ka iya:

- Kara sabon listing.
- Gyara bayanin listing.
- Kara hotuna.
- Canza farashi.
- Saka condition da location.
- Nuna hanyar pickup ko delivery.
- Canza matsayin listing idan an sayar da kaya.

Ka yi amfani da taken listing mai bayyana kaya kai tsaye. Misali, maimakon **Phone**, yi amfani da **Samsung Galaxy A15 128GB**. Hoto mai kyau da cikakken bayani suna taimaka wa buyer ya yanke shawara.

### 5.3 Customer Messages

Danna **Customer messages** domin bude saƙonnin buyers. Wannan yana kai ka zuwa inbox na Bese26, inda za ka iya:

- Karanta buyer inquiry.
- Amsa sakon buyer.
- Tura hoto ko voice note idan ya dace.
- Tabbatar da availability na kaya.
- Ba buyer karin bayani game da farashi, pickup, ko delivery.

Ka yi kokarin amsa cikin lokaci. Amsa mai sauri tana kara amincewar buyer.

### 5.4 Storefront Editor

Danna **Storefront editor** domin gyara bayanan da buyers suke gani a public business page. Form din da ke kasa yana ba ka damar gyara:

- Business name.
- Business handle.
- Business type da category.
- Logo.
- Description.
- Phone da WhatsApp.
- Contact preference.
- Country, state, city, area, da address.
- Location visibility.
- Website.
- Years in business.
- Registration number.
- Delivery da pickup settings.
- Public contact setting.

Bayan ka yi canji, danna **Save business profile**. Canjin zai bayyana a storefront bayan an sabunta shafin.

### 5.5 Share Storefront

Danna **Share storefront** ko **Copy store link** domin samun official link na business dinka. Link din zai kasance a kan domain na Bese26:

```text
https://www.bese26.shop/@your-handle
```

Za ka iya tura link din ga customers ta WhatsApp, Facebook, SMS, ko sauran hanyoyin sadarwa. Kada ka yi amfani da Vercel link wajen raba shagonka.

### 5.6 Verification

Danna **Verification** domin fara ko duba tsarin verification. Kafin ka fara, ka cika muhimman business details kamar suna, handle, category, description, email, phone, state, da city.

| Matsayi | Ma’anarsa |
|---|---|
| Not started | Ba a fara verification ba |
| Draft | An fara amma ba a kammala ba |
| Pending review | An tura bayanai ana jiran dubawa |
| Needs information | Ana bukatar karin bayani |
| Verified | An amince da business |
| Rejected | Ba a amince da application ba |
| Suspended | An dakatar da visibility na business |

Verification ba ya faruwa kai tsaye saboda kawai an kirkiri business. Masu kula da Bese26 ne ke duba bayanan kuma su yanke hukunci.

### 5.7 Subscription

Sashen subscription yana nuna plan din account da matsayin Business tools. Idan kana amfani da wani plan, danna **Manage subscription** domin duba bayanin plan. Idan ba ka da Business plan, za ka iya ganin **View business plans** ko **Unlock Business tools** bisa matsayin account dinka.

## 6. Yadda public storefront yake aiki

Public storefront page shi ne shafin da buyers za su gani ba tare da login ba. Yana iya nuna:

- Business logo da cover.
- Business name da handle.
- Welcome message ko description.
- Wurin business.
- Opening hours idan an saita su.
- Pickup da delivery options.
- Personal profile linked to business.
- Duk listings na business.
- **Call**, **WhatsApp**, da **Message** buttons a kowane listing, idan bayanan tuntuba suna nan.

Misali, idan handle din business shi ne `mekano`, buyers za su bude:

```text
https://www.bese26.shop/@mekano
```

## 7. Shawarwari domin storefront ya burge buyers

Ka yi amfani da logo mai haske da quality mai kyau. Ka rubuta description da zai bayyana abin da kake sayarwa, wurin da kake aiki, da irin sabis din da kake bayarwa. Ka saka farashi da hotunan kaya masu kyau. Ka sabunta listing idan an sayar da kaya domin kada buyer ya tuntube ka a kan abin da ba ya nan.

Ka tabbatar cewa lambar WhatsApp da phone sun yi daidai kafin ka kunna buttons din tuntuba. Idan ba ka son a nuna lambar waya a fili, yi amfani da contact preference da public contact settings da suka dace da manufarka.

## 8. Tsaro da ikon mai amfani

Business Admin yana aiki ne da account din da ya mallaki business. Mai business zai iya gyara business dinsa da listings dinsa, amma ba zai iya sarrafa bayanan wani business ba.

Kada ka ba wani password ko verification information na account dinka. Idan ka ga wani abu da ba ka gane ba, ka fita daga account din kuma ka sake shiga daga official domain na Bese26.

## 9. Matsalolin da aka fi samu da mafita

| Matsala | Mafita |
|---|---|
| Ba ka ga Business Admin ba | Ka shiga account din da ya kirkiri business profile |
| Handle din ya riga ya kasance | Canza handle zuwa wanda bai riga ya kasance ba |
| Public page bai bude ba | Duba cewa business handle da `is_active` profile suna daidai; sannan ka sake bude official link |
| WhatsApp button bai fito ba | Saka WhatsApp number kuma duba contact preference |
| Call button bai fito ba | Saka phone number kuma duba public contact settings |
| Listing bai bayyana ba | Duba listing status da moderation status; listing mai active ne zai fi dacewa ya bayyana |
| Analytics ba su karu ba | Alkaluman suna iya sabuntawa bayan an samu sababbin views ko inquiries |
| Logo bai canza ba | Danna Save business profile bayan upload, sannan ka sake refresh shafin |

- [ ] Business name ya cika.
- [ ] Public handle yana samuwa.
- [ ] Logo ya kasance.
- [ ] Description ya bayyana abin da business ke yi.
- [ ] State da city sun cika.
- [ ] Phone ko WhatsApp da ake so an saka.
- [ ] Contact preference ya dace.
- [ ] Delivery ko pickup an saita.
- [ ] Akalla listing daya tana active.
- [ ] Public storefront link yana budewa.
- [ ] Call, WhatsApp, da Message buttons sun nuna yadda ake so.

## 11. Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

### Shin ina bukatar sabon account domin business?

A'a. Personal account dinka shi ne owner account. Business Admin yana aiki a kan account guda.

### Shin zan iya samun business fiye da daya?

Wannan ya dogara da dokokin account da tsarin Bese26. Idan kana bukatar karin business profile, ka bi tsarin da app ya nuna ko ka tuntubi masu kula da Bese26.

### Shin buyers suna bukatar login domin ganin storefront?

A'a. Public storefront da public listings ana iya budewa ba tare da buyer ya shiga login ba.

### Shin zan iya boye phone number?

Eh. Yi amfani da contact preferences da public contact setting. Za ka iya barin Message ko WhatsApp kawai idan ba ka son a nuna Call.

### Me zan yi idan na manta store link?

Bude **Business Admin**, sannan danna **Copy store link**. Za a kwafi official link na Bese26.

### Wanene zai iya gyara business?

Mai account da ya kirkiri business ne kawai zai iya gyara shi daga Business Admin.
