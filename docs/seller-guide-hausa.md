# Bese26 Seller Guide

## Jagorar Sellers kan Plans, Verification da Boosts

**Sigar jagora:** 1.0  
**Ranar sabuntawa:** 9 Satumba 2026  
**Wanda aka shirya wa:** Sellers na Bese26.shop

---

## 1. Gabatarwa

Bese26 marketplace ne na Najeriya da ke haɗa masu saye da masu sayarwa domin sayar da kayayyaki, ayyuka, da kayan kasuwanci.[1] Seller zai iya fara sayarwa kyauta, sannan ya haɓaka zuwa plan mai girma idan yana buƙatar ƙarin active listings, business tools, verification eligibility, ko free boost credits.

> **Muhimmin bayani:** Biyan plan yana buɗe damar amfani da features na plan. Ba ya bada tabbacin sayarwa, yawan buyers, ko automatic approval na verification.

Wannan jagorar ta bayyana abin da kowane plan yake bayarwa, yadda ake yin verification, yadda boost credits suke aiki, da abin da zai faru idan wata ta ƙare.

## 2. Ma’anar kalmomin da ake amfani da su

| Kalma | Ma’ana |

| **Active listing** | Kayan da yake live kuma buyers za su iya gani a marketplace. |

## 4. Free plan

Free plan yana ba sabon seller damar fara amfani da marketplace ba tare da biyan kuɗi ba. Yana da **3 active listings**, basic seller profile, Bese26 chat, saved items, da seller following.

Free plan bai buɗe verification ba kuma bai bada free boost credits ba. Idan seller ya kai active listings uku, zai iya gyara ko ya dakatar da wani listing, ko ya zaɓi plan mafi girma domin ƙara capacity.

## 5. Basic plan — ₦2,999 a wata

Basic plan ya dace da seller mai ƙananan kaya da yake son ƙarin listing capacity. Yana bada **15 active listings** da sauran basic marketplace features.

Basic plan bai buɗe verification ba kuma ba ya bada monthly free boost credits. Seller na Basic zai iya amfani da marketplace da chat, amma idan yana son verification ko free boosts, sai ya haɓaka zuwa Premium ko Business.

## 6. Premium plan — ₦7,000 a wata

Premium shi ne plan da ya dace da seller mai kayan sayarwa akai-akai. Yana bada **35 active listings** da manyan seller tools.

Premium seller yana samun abubuwa kamar haka:

- Advanced seller analytics da bayanin yadda buyers suke hulɗa da listings.
- Business profile tools da ingantaccen seller dashboard.
- Damar fara verification application.
- **Free boost credits guda 5 kowane wata.**
- Bese26 chat, saved items, seller following, da listing management.
- Ƙarin capacity fiye da Free da Basic.

Boost credits na Premium ba su nufin cewa duk listings za su zama featured har abada ba. Kowane free boost yana aiki ne bisa package da tsarin boosting na Bese26.

## 7. Business plan — ₦14,999 a wata

Business plan an tsara shi ne domin professional business, company, retailer, wholesaler, agribusiness, service provider, da sauran businesses masu kaya da yawa. Yana bada **60 active listings**.

Business seller yana samun:

- Business Center mai storefront management.
- Public business page da business handle/link.
- Logo, business identity, category, contact preferences, delivery, da pickup settings.
- Business analytics da listing management.
- Damar fara business verification application.
- **Free boost credits guda 10 kowane wata.**
- Customer messages da kayan taimaka wa business wajen tafiyar da store.

Business plan ba ya nufin cewa business ya samu verified badge kai tsaye. Verification har yanzu yana buƙatar submission da review daga Bese26 moderation.

## 8. Yadda ake haɓaka plan

Seller zai iya bin wannan hanya:

1. Shiga account ɗinsa na Bese26.
2. Buɗe **Profile**.
3. Je zuwa **Payments & Services**.
4. Buɗe **Subscription** ko **Seller Plans**.
5. Danna **More** domin ganin cikakken benefits na plan.
6. Zaɓi **Basic**, **Premium**, ko **Business**.
7. Duba farashi da abubuwan da plan ɗin zai bayar.
8. Danna **Choose plan**.
9. Bi Paystack checkout.
10. Jira app ya tabbatar da payment kafin plan ya fara aiki.

> Kada seller ya ɗauka cewa ya biya ne kawai saboda Paystack checkout ya buɗe. Plan zai fara aiki ne bayan Bese26 server ya tabbatar da payment cikin aminci.

## 9. Verification: yadda yake aiki

Verification na taimakawa buyers su gane account ko business da aka duba. Ana iya samun identity verification, seller verification, ko business verification bisa bayanan da seller ya submit.

### Wa ya cancanci verification?

Premium da Business ne kawai suke da damar fara verification application. Free da Basic idan suka danna verification, app zai kai su Seller Plans domin su zaɓi Premium ko Business.

### Matakan verification

1. Haɓaka account zuwa **Premium ₦7,000/wata** ko **Business ₦14,999/wata**.
2. Buɗe **Verification & Trust** ko Business Center.
3. Idan business ne, tabbatar an cika business details.
4. Zaɓi **Start Verification**.
5. Cika bayanan da ake buƙata sau ɗaya.
6. Tura application da supporting documents idan ana buƙata.
7. Jira Bese26 moderation ta duba application.
8. Bayan an amince, verified status zai bayyana a inda ya dace.

### Business details da ake buƙata

Business seller ya kamata ya tanadi:

- Business name.
- Public handle.
- Business type.
- Business category.
- Business description.
- Business email.
- Business phone.
- State.
- Local Government Area.
- Business address ko registration information idan ana buƙata.

### Shin sai an sake cike form?

A’a, idan seller ya riga ya tura verification application kuma status ɗinsa yana **Pending Review**, **Under Review**, **Approved**, ko **Verified**, ba zai sake cike form daga farko ba. Bayan ya sabunta Premium ko Business plan, tsohuwar application ɗinsa za ta ci gaba da amfani da ita.

Idan an dawo da application saboda ƙarin bayani ko an ƙi ta, seller zai ga abin da moderator ya nema. A wannan yanayin ne kawai zai gyara ko ƙara bayanai kafin ya sake submit.

### Abin da verification ba ta nufi ba

Verification ba ta nufin cewa Bese26 ta ba seller tabbacin sales, buyers, riba, ko transaction safety a kowane hali. Buyers su ci gaba da bincika kaya, farashi, wurin haɗuwa, da bayanan seller cikin hankali.

## 10. Verification da ƙarshen subscription

Verification access yana da alaƙa da active Premium ko Business plan.

| Yanayin account | Sakamako |
|---|---|
| Premium ko Business yana aiki | Verified access da badge suna aiki bisa approval status. |
| Subscription ya ƙare | Verification access da verified badge suna ɓoyewa. |
| Seller ya sabunta Premium ko Business | Tsohuwar approved verification za ta dawo idan ba a soke ta ba. |
| Seller ya koma Free ko Basic | Ba zai iya submit sabon verification ba. |
| Moderator ya soke ko ya dakatar da verification | Badge ba zai bayyana ba ko da plan yana aiki. |

> Subscription ba ya goge verification application ko business details. Yana dakatar da access da badge ne har sai seller ya koma eligible plan.

## 11. Monthly free boost credits

Premium yana samun **5 free boost credits** a kowane wata. Business yana samun **10 free boost credits** a kowane wata. Free da Basic ba su samun waɗannan credits.

Don amfani da credit:

1. Je zuwa **Profile → Payments & Services → Boosting**.
2. Zaɓi approved active listing.
3. Idan kana da credit, danna **Use free boost**.
4. App zai rage credit ɗaya bayan an yi amfani da shi.
5. Boost zai fara bisa tsarin Bese26 boost package.

Free boost credits ba su canzawa zuwa kuɗi kuma ba a mayar da su kuɗi. Credits na watan da ya wuce ba sa taruwa da na sabon wata. Idan plan ya ƙare, credits da suka rage ba za a iya amfani da su ba.

Paid boost dabam yake da free monthly credit. Seller zai iya biyan boost ta Paystack idan yana son amfani da paid boost package bayan ya zaɓi listing da package.

## 12. Active listings da iyaka

Active listing shi ne listing da yake live a marketplace. Draft, rejected, sold, archived, ko paused listing ba sa ƙidayuwa a matsayin active listing har sai an mayar da su active.

Idan seller ya kai limit:

- Ba za a goge tsoffin listings ba.
- Ba zai iya ƙara sabbin active listings fiye da limit ba.
- Zai iya gyara, pause, mark as sold, ko archive listing.
- Zai iya haɓaka plan domin samun ƙarin capacity.

Listing limits na yanzu su ne:

```text
Free     = 3 active listings
Basic    = 15 active listings
Premium  = 35 active listings
Business = 60 active listings
```

## 13. Business Center

Business Center yana ba business seller wuri guda da zai kula da storefront ɗinsa. Idan business details ba su cika ba, form zai bayyana domin a fara setup.

Bayan an ajiye bayanan, Business Center zai nuna summary kamar business name, handle, category, da location. Form ɗin ba zai sake cika screen ba. Seller zai danna **Edit business details** ne kawai idan yana son canza bayanai.

Business Center yana taimakawa wajen:

- Kula da public business page.
- Ƙara ko gyara business logo.
- Sarrafa active listings.
- Duba store analytics.
- Karɓar buyer messages.
- Raba public storefront link.
- Shirya verification readiness.

## 14. Dokokin posting mai kyau

Seller ya kamata ya saka hotuna masu kyau, title mai bayyana kaya, farashi mai gaskiya, location daidai, da cikakken description. A guji saka bayanan karya, hoton da ba naka ba, ko kaya da marketplace ya hana.

Kafin ka publish listing, ka tabbatar cewa:

- Category ta dace da kayan.
- Farashi yana a NGN kuma ba shi da kuskure.
- Location da contact preference sun dace.
- Hoton farko yana bayyana ainihin kayan.
- Description ya bayyana condition da abin da buyer zai samu.
- Ba a saka password, secret key, ko sensitive document a public description ba.

## 15. Payment da matsalolin checkout

Duk plan da paid boost suna buƙatar Paystack checkout. Bese26 server ne kawai yake tabbatar da payment. Kada seller ya tura Paystack secret key, Supabase service-role key, ko wani API key ga wani mutum.

Idan payment ta kasa:

1. Kada ka sake biyan kuɗi sau da yawa ba tare da duba transaction history ba.
2. Duba ko Paystack checkout ya nuna successful payment.
3. Duba **Payment History** a Bese26.
4. Idan an cire kuɗi amma plan bai canza ba, adana payment reference.
5. Tuntuɓi Bese26 support ta `info@bese26.shop` tare da email ɗin account, plan, ranar payment, da reference.

## 16. Tambayoyi da amsoshi

### Zan iya fara sayarwa ba tare da plan ba?

Eh. Free plan yana baka active listings guda uku.

### Wane plan ne ake buƙata domin verification?

Premium na ₦7,000 a wata ko Business na ₦14,999 a wata.

### Premium ya fi Basic da me?

Premium yana da 35 active listings, verification eligibility, advanced seller tools, da free boost credits guda biyar a wata.

### Business ya fi Premium da me?

Business yana da 60 active listings, professional storefront tools, business management features, verification eligibility, da free boost credits guda goma a wata.

### Verification approval automatic ne bayan na biya?

A’a. Biyan plan yana buɗe damar submit verification. Bese26 moderation ce ke duba application ta yanke hukunci.

### Zan sake cike verification bayan na sabunta plan?

A’a, idan an riga an ajiye ko an approve application ɗinka. Za a ci gaba da amfani da tsohuwar application muddin ba a nemi ƙarin bayani ba.

### Me zai faru idan wata ta ƙare?

Paid plan features, verification access, verified badge, da free boost credits za su daina aiki har sai an sabunta plan. Existing listings ba za a goge ba.

### Shin free boost yana nufin guaranteed buyers?

A’a. Boost yana ƙara visibility ne kawai. Ba ya bada tabbacin buyers ko sales.
